Kurbi — Privacy Policy OUTLINE (structure + required content, not final prose)


Version: 0.1-outline • For: [Kurbi, LLC], Florida • Scope: iOS app, web, AI phone line.


1. Who we are / scope

- Controller: [Kurbi, LLC], [address], [privacy contact email].

- Covers: Kurbi app, website, AI phone line, provider tools. Does NOT cover providers' own independent processing of customer data once a booking connects them (providers are independent controllers of what customers give them directly) — state this plainly.


2. Data we collect

- Account: name, email, phone, password hash, role (customer/provider), profile photo.

- Provider business data: business name, categories, prices, license/permit uploads, insurance certificates (insurer, policy no., limits, expiry), payout status flags from Stripe.

- Booking data: service type, date/time, price, notes, messages between customer and provider, dispute-center submissions and evidence uploads.

- Location:

- Customer service address — collected for bookings; shared only with the provider the customer actually books (not with browsing providers; approximate area only pre-booking). [Confirm product behavior matches before publishing.]

- Provider en-route location sharing — opt-in per session, provider toggles it when departing; stops at arrival/completion; not collected in the background outside an active share.

- AI phone line: call audio recordings, transcripts, caller ID, booking intents extracted by the assistant.

- Consent: disclosure at call start; all-party-consent states — Florida is one — require consent before recording; describe the consent mechanism (continued participation after disclosure / IVR prompt).

- Payments: processed by Stripe; Kurbi does not collect or store full card numbers; Kurbi sees tokens, last4, transaction metadata. Stripe acts under its own privacy policy (link).

- Photos/media: profile photos, portfolio images, before/after shots, dispute evidence.

- Device & analytics: device identifiers, app version, crash logs, usage analytics [name the SDKs when chosen], IP addresses (including IP captured at ToS acceptance for record-keeping).


3. How we use data

- Operate the marketplace (matching, booking, scheduling, payments via Stripe).

- Run the AI phone line (answer calls, create bookings, quality and accuracy review of the automated assistant). State whether recordings/transcripts are used to improve/tune the AI system and whether vendors may use them — see §4.

- Trust & integrity: fraud prevention, dispute facilitation, badge verification, enforcing terms.

- Communications: booking notifications, service messages; marketing only with consent/opt-out.

- Legal compliance, record-keeping (ToS acceptance logs), safety.
