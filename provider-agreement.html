Kurbi — Provider Agreement (DRAFT)


Version: 0.1-draft • Effective Date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] • Entity: [KURBI, LLC — a Florida limited liability company]


This Provider Agreement ("Agreement") is between [Kurbi, LLC] ("Kurbi") and the independent business or individual professional accepting it ("Provider," "you"). It governs your use of the Kurbi platform, apps, AI phone line, and related tools (the "Platform") to offer mobile services to customers ("Customers").


PLEASE READ SECTION 13 (ARBITRATION; CLASS ACTION WAIVER). IT REQUIRES INDIVIDUAL ARBITRATION OF DISPUTES UNLESS YOU OPT OUT WITHIN 30 DAYS.



1. Relationship of the Parties — Independent Contractor


1.1 Independent business. You are an independent business providing services directly to Customers. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership, joint-venture, or franchise relationship between you and Kurbi. You are not an employee or agent of Kurbi and may not hold yourself out as one.


1.2 You control your work. You alone determine:

(a) your prices — all service prices, travel fees, and cancellation fees on your profile are set and entered by you;

(b) your methods, techniques, and standards of performing services;

(c) your tools, equipment, products, and supplies;

(d) your schedule, availability, service area, and which bookings to accept or decline; and

(e) whether and when to use optional Platform tools (Section 5.4).


> [DRAFTING NOTE — load-bearing for classification]: For 1.2(a) to hold, the app's price fields must be provider-entered, never pre-filled, suggested-at-entry, or set by Kurbi. Displaying market ranges as neutral information is a judgment call for counsel; auto-populating a price is not. See app-checklist §2.


1.3 No exclusivity. You may offer services outside the Platform, use competing platforms, and serve your own clients. Kurbi does not restrict your other work. Bookings originated through the Platform are subject to this Agreement, including Platform fees.


1.4 Your personnel. If you use employees or subcontractors, they are yours alone: you are responsible for their compensation, supervision, taxes, insurance, and legal compliance, and this Agreement's obligations apply to work they perform under your account.


2. No Employment Benefits; Taxes


2.1 No benefits. You are not entitled to wages, overtime, workers' compensation, unemployment insurance, health insurance, retirement, or any other employment benefits from Kurbi.


2.2 Taxes are yours. You are solely responsible for all income, self-employment, sales, and other taxes on your earnings, and for any required registrations and filings. Payment reporting (e.g., Form 1099-K) is issued by the payment processor where legally required, based on thresholds set by law. Kurbi does not withhold taxes.


3. Licenses, Permits, and Insurance


3.1 Credentials. You represent and warrant that you hold, and will maintain for the term of this Agreement, every license, permit, registration, and certification required by federal, state, county, or municipal law for the services you offer (e.g., barbering/cosmetology licensure, pesticide applicator permits, local business tax receipts), and that you will provide services only in categories and locations where you are legally permitted to work. You will upload proof upon request and notify Kurbi promptly if a credential lapses or is revoked.


3.2 Insurance. You are strongly advised to carry, and for certain categories or badges may be required to carry, commercial general liability insurance appropriate to your trade — recommended minimum [US $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate] — plus any legally required coverage (e.g., workers' compensation for your employees, commercial auto for your vehicle).


3.3 Verification is limited. Kurbi may review uploaded documents for facial completeness (named insured, coverage line, limits, expiry) but does not verify authenticity with issuers unless stated, and assumes no duty to Customers or to you arising from such review. See Section 9 (Insured badge).


4. Your Services; Compliance


4.1 You are solely responsible for the services you provide: their quality, safety, legality, scheduling, performance, pricing, and any commitments you make to Customers. The service contract is between you and the Customer; Kurbi is not a party to it.


4.2 You will comply with all applicable laws in performing services, including consumer-protection, licensing, safety, anti-discrimination, and animal-welfare laws, and you will not provide services while impaired.


5. Marketplace Integrity Rules (Not Operational Control)


5.1 Purpose. The rules in this Section exist to keep the marketplace honest and bookings reliable for everyone. They govern conduct on the Platform (accuracy, honoring confirmed bookings, non-abuse); they do not direct how you perform your trade.


5.2 Integrity rules. You agree to: (a) keep your profile, credentials, prices, and availability accurate; (b) honor confirmed bookings or cancel them through the app as early as practicable; (c) not manipulate ratings or reviews (no fake, purchased, or coerced reviews); (d) not misrepresent credentials, insurance, or identity; (e) treat Customers lawfully and without harassment or discrimination; (f) not use the Platform for unlawful services.


5.3 Ratings and repeated no-shows. Customers may rate completed services. Chronic no-shows, confirmed fraud, or sustained integrity-rule violations may lead to reduced visibility, suspension, or termination under Section 14. Kurbi does not set quality standards for your trade and does not penalize you for your prices, methods, schedule, or decisions to decline bookings.


> [DRAFTING NOTE — classification tension]: Every rule in §5 is framed as marketplace integrity rather than operational control, which matters for independent-contractor status.


5.4 Optional tools are tools, not mandates. The Platform offers optional tools — including the travel-time auto-scheduler (which proposes booking slots based on drive time between your appointments) and the AI phone line (Section 6). You choose whether to enable each tool, may disable it at any time, and may override any slot, route, or suggestion it produces. Declining or disabling a tool does not affect your standing, fees, or visibility.


> [DRAFTING NOTE — classification tension]: The auto-scheduler must remain genuinely opt-in and overridable in the product.


6. AI Phone Line


6.1 If you enable the AI phone line, you authorize Kurbi's automated assistant to answer calls on your business line or Kurbi-provisioned number and, as your limited-purpose tool, to schedule, modify, or cancel appointments in your calendar according to the availability, prices, and rules you configure. You may review, change, or cancel any AI-made booking.


6.2 Calls may be recorded and transcribed where permitted by law; the assistant discloses recording and its automated nature at the start of each call. You are responsible for any additional notice your own jurisdiction or business requires when the line is held out as your business number.


7. Payments — Stripe Connect; Provider as Merchant of Record


7.1 Processing. Customer payments are processed through Stripe Connect. To receive payouts you must create and maintain a Stripe connected account and accept Stripe's Connected Account Agreement, including the Stripe Services Agreement. You are the merchant of record for each service transaction; Kurbi provides platform and payment-facilitation technology.


7.2 Platform fees. Kurbi deducts its platform fee/commission as disclosed in the app fee schedule at the time of booking. Kurbi may update the fee schedule with [30] days' notice; continued acceptance of new bookings after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated schedule.


7.3 Taxes on sales. As merchant of record, you are responsible for determining, collecting, and remitting any sales or service taxes due on your services.


7.4 Chargebacks. As merchant of record, you bear chargebacks, reversals, and associated processor fees for your transactions, except to the extent caused by a Platform processing error. Kurbi may deduct chargeback amounts from pending payouts or invoice you. You agree to respond to chargeback evidence requests promptly; Kurbi may assist with evidence from Platform records.


8. Refunds and the Dispute Center


8.1 Customer complaints are routed through the in-app Dispute Center. You agree to respond to opened disputes within [3] business days.


8.2 Refunds for your services are your decision and are issued from your transaction proceeds. You may authorize Kurbi to initiate refunds through Stripe on your instruction, or automatically under a refund policy you adopt in your settings. Kurbi facilitates the process and does not adjudicate service quality.


9. "Insured" Badge Conditions


9.1 The Insured badge may be displayed on your profile only while all of the following are true: (a) you uploaded a certificate of insurance naming you/your business as insured; (b) the document shows GL coverage at or above the Platform minimum for your category; (c) the policy is unexpired; and (d) the document passed Kurbi's facial review.


9.2 The badge is removed automatically upon policy expiry or upon notice of cancellation, and restored when a current certificate is verified. You must notify Kurbi within [5] days if coverage lapses, is cancelled, or materially changes.


9.3 Misrepresenting insurance is a material breach and grounds for immediate termination. The badge communicates document submission status only; it is not a representation by Kurbi to Customers that coverage exists or will pay a claim, and in-app badge copy will say so.


10. Indemnification of Kurbi


To the maximum extent permitted by law, you will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Kurbi and its officers, members, employees, and agents from and against all claims, damages, liabilities, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to: (a) your services, including any property damage, personal injury, or death arising from their performance; (b) your breach of this Agreement or violation of law; (c) your taxes, employees, or subcontractors; (d) your content (Section 15); or (e) misclassification claims premised on your or your personnel's work, except in each case to the extent caused by Kurbi's own gross negligence or willful misconduct. Kurbi will promptly notify you of claims and may participate with its own counsel.

13. Arbitration; Class Action Waiver


13.1 Informal resolution first. Before arbitration, the parties will attempt good-faith resolution for 30 days after written notice of the dispute to [NOTICE EMAIL / ADDRESS].


13.2 Binding individual arbitration. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Platform will be resolved by final and binding arbitration on an individual basis, administered by the AAA under its Commercial Arbitration Rules (or, if the AAA determines a provider qualifies as a consumer, its Consumer Arbitration Rules), seated in [Miami-Dade County], Florida. The Federal Arbitration Act governs this Section.


13.3 Carve-outs. Either party may bring an individual claim in small-claims court, or seek injunctive relief in court for intellectual-property infringement or misuse of confidential information.


13.4 CLASS ACTION WAIVER. BOTH PARTIES WAIVE THE RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL AND TO PARTICIPATE IN ANY CLASS, COLLECTIVE, CONSOLIDATED, OR REPRESENTATIVE PROCEEDING. If this waiver is unenforceable for a claim, that claim proceeds in court and the remainder stays in arbitration.


13.5 30-day opt-out. You may opt out of this Section by written notice to [OPT-OUT EMAIL / ADDRESS] within 30 days of first accepting this Agreement, stating your name, business name, account email, and intent to opt out.


13.6 This Section survives termination.


14. Term and Termination


14.1 This Agreement starts when you accept it and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate at any time, with or without cause, by notice (you: by closing your provider account; Kurbi: by written/in-app notice). Kurbi may suspend or terminate immediately for fraud, safety risk, credential or insurance misrepresentation, or material breach.


14.2 On termination: you will complete or cancel (through the app) bookings already confirmed; earned payouts are settled per Stripe timelines, subject to holds for chargeback exposure; Sections 1–2 (status), 7.4 (chargebacks), 10–13, 15.2, and 16 survive.

You grant Kurbi a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license to host, reproduce, display, and distribute Provider Content for operating, promoting, and improving the Platform, during the term and for a reasonable wind-down period thereafter. You represent you have all rights to the Provider Content (including client-photo permissions) and that it is accurate and lawful.
